# Application notification icons

Semaphore, Sonarr, Radarr, Metabase and GitHub marks were obtained from
[Homarr Labs dashboard-icons](https://github.com/homarr-labs/dashboard-icons)
(`png/semaphore.png`, `sonarr.png`, `radarr.png`, `metabase.png`, `github.png`)
on 2026-10-02 and resized to at most 256 pixels for packaging. Product trademarks
remain the property of their respective owners; no endorsement is implied.
The upstream project license and attribution are available in its repository.

- Checkmk: official website icon, https://checkmk.com/android-chrome-192x192.png. Product identification only.

- GitHub Actions active icon: `github-actions-white.png` is the unchanged official
  `PNG/GitHub_Invertocat_White.png` from GitHub's
  [logo package](https://brand.github.com/GitHub_Logos.zip), obtained 2026-10-15.
  The white variant follows the [official color guidance](https://brand.github.com/foundations/logo)
  for legibility on dark surfaces. The previous Homarr-derived `github-actions.png`
  remains packaged for existing card URLs.

- QNAP active icon: `qnap-white.png` is the unchanged official
  `logo_QNAP_LOGO_K0.png` from the vendor's
  [white logo PNG package](https://marketing.qnap.com/download/750/?version=logo-png),
  obtained 2026-10-15. The [resource page](https://marketing.qnap.com/resource/qnap-logo-k0/)
  identifies the white variant as RGB 255, 255, 255.
- Synology active icon: `synology-white.png` is the unchanged official
  `Synology_logo_white.png` from the vendor's
  [logo package](https://design.synology.com/assets/guidelines/resources/downloads/01_logo_synology_logo_package.zip),
  obtained 2026-10-15. Its [logo guidance](https://design.synology.com/elements/logo)
  specifies the white variant for dark backgrounds.
  Previous `qnap.png` and `synology.png` assets remain packaged for existing URLs.
  Slack Classic uses `qnap-white-slack.png` and `synology-white-slack.png`:
  the unchanged white artwork centered on a transparent square canvas, so
  Slack's square cover crop retains the whole wordmark. Other providers keep
  the native wide assets.

- Supermicro active icon: `supermicro-white-fill.png` is the unchanged official
  [PNG White Background](https://www.supermicro.com/sites/default/files/content_resources/static_resources/logos/Supermicro_GreenC_NewLogo_WhiteBackground.png)
  linked by the [vendor logo page](https://www.supermicro.com/en/supermicro-logos),
  obtained 2026-10-15. The [2026 Rev.4 brand guide](https://www.supermicro.com/manuals/SUPERMICRO_Logo_Guidelines.pdf),
  pages 16–18, requires white center fill inside the ellipse. Native palette
  transparency and artwork are preserved. Previous `supermicro.png` and
  `discord/supermicro.png` assets remain packaged for existing URLs.
